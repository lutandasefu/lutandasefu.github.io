07 · Logo Construction

The Boercorp logo is built on a foundation of geometric precision and intentional proportion. Each element — the circular seal, brandmark illustration, script logotype, and consortium ring text — occupies a defined zone within the construction grid, ensuring consistent reproduction across all scales and applications.

Zone 1 — Outer Ring

The circular border and consortium text form the outer boundary. This ring provides structure and frames all inner elements.

Zone 2 — Brandmark

The farm illustration occupies the upper centre, anchoring the visual identity in agricultural heritage and community.

Zone 3 — Script Logotype

The Boercorp script sits centrally below the brandmark, forming the focal point of the seal with expressive, flowing letterforms.

Zone 4 — Proportions

Carefully balanced proportions create a harmonious and scalable identity that reads clearly from small to billboard scale.

08 · Clear Space & Minimum Size

To preserve the integrity and legibility of the Boercorp logo, a defined clear space must always be maintained around the mark. No other graphic elements, text, or imagery should encroach within this protected zone.

Clear Space Rule

The minimum clear space on all sides equals the height of the letter "B" in the Boercorp script. This applies to both the seal and horizontal lockup.

Minimum Size

The seal logo should never be reproduced smaller than the equivalent of 24mm in print or 72px digitally to ensure legibility.

One Day Sopopa

Brand Identity · 2024

Mens Clothing · Youth Brand

One Day Sopopa

Dream now · Achieve one day

Overview

One Day Sopopa is a mens clothing brand founded by a young entrepreneur, created for young men growing up in rural areas across South Africa. The brand carries a simple but powerful message — that dreams, no matter how distant they feel today, can be achieved one day. The visual identity speaks directly to ambition, resilience, and the quiet confidence of someone who knows their time is coming.

The Story

Born from the lived experience of growing up in a rural town with big dreams and limited resources — One Day Sopopa exists to remind young men that ambition has no postcode.

The Audience

Young men in rural and township communities who carry big dreams — the brand speaks their language and wears their story on every garment.

The Tone

Bold, raw, and hopeful — a brand voice that feels like a friend reminding you to keep going, dressed in streetwear that wears the message with pride.

The Vision

To grow from a local rural label into a recognised national streetwear brand — proving that the "one day" promise can become today's reality.

Custom Typography

A custom hand-crafted typeface designed specifically for One Day Sopopa — bold, rugged letterforms that mirror the resilience and grit of the brand's story.

Logo Evolution

The journey from initial concept to final mark — three transitional stages refining the logo into its final, confident form.

Colour Palette

A high-contrast palette of deep blue and fierce red against black and white — communicating ambition, confidence, and unapologetic self-belief.
