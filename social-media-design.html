The Brand

Dough Jo is a one-of-a-kind lifestyle brand that refuses to choose between indulgence and discipline. A doughnut shop and gym under one roof — the social media identity was designed to celebrate both sides of that duality. Bold visuals, playful energy, and a distinctive colour palette make every post unmistakably Dough Jo.

The Concept

A feed that lives in the tension between sweet and sweat — indulgent photography balanced with energetic gym content, all tied together by a consistent visual language that makes the brand feel cohesive and alive.

The Feed Strategy

Posts alternate between product shots, lifestyle imagery, and community content — creating a rhythm that keeps followers engaged and the brand feeling dynamic rather than repetitive.

The Tone

Playful, confident, and a little cheeky — copy and visuals work together to build a brand personality that feels genuine, community-driven, and impossible to scroll past.

The Platform

Designed primarily for Instagram — optimised for square feed posts and vertical reels, with a consistent aesthetic that translates seamlessly across stories, highlights, and carousel formats.
