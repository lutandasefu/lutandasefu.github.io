About Zaphub

At Zaphub, the mission is to make real estate as straightforward, empowering, and accessible as possible. As South Africa's innovative, all-in-one real estate platform, Zaphub caters to property buyers, sellers, and professionals who seek a seamless, transparent experience. The marketing collateral was designed to translate this mission into visually compelling communication — simplifying complex property transactions and making them feel smarter, easier, and more rewarding for everyone involved.

The Brief

Design a suite of marketing flyers that communicate Zaphub's value proposition clearly and compellingly — speaking to buyers, sellers, and property professionals across digital and print channels.

The Approach

Each flyer was designed with a clear hierarchy — leading with a bold headline, supporting with concise copy, and closing with a strong call to action. Consistency across the suite reinforces brand recognition.

The Audience

Property buyers seeking their first home, sellers looking for maximum exposure, and real estate professionals wanting a smarter platform to manage and close transactions efficiently.

The Outcome

A cohesive set of marketing assets that feel modern, trustworthy, and distinctly Zaphub — ready for deployment across social media, email campaigns, and physical print distribution.
