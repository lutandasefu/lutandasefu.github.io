Impact

Clean · Bold · Powerful

Overview

Impact is a washing powder brand designed to command attention. The brief called for a product identity that felt powerful, trustworthy, and visually distinctive on a crowded retail shelf. The design system — from logo to packaging — was built around contrast, boldness, and a colour palette that communicates energy and efficacy without compromise.

The Concept

The name Impact drives every design decision. Bold letterforms, high-contrast colour blocking, and dynamic layout create a visual language that communicates strength and reliability at a glance.

The Typography

The Alpha typeface anchors the logotype — a bold, geometric sans-serif that conveys confidence and modernity. Its strong presence ensures legibility across all packaging sizes and formats.

The Packaging

Designed for real-world shelf impact — the front and back packaging layouts balance brand expression with functional information, ensuring clarity without sacrificing visual power.

The Market

Positioned for the everyday South African household — a product that feels premium and purposeful without being inaccessible. Designed to earn trust through consistency and confidence.

Logo Variations

The Impact logo was developed across three colour expressions — each maintaining the same bold typographic foundation while adapting to different background contexts and applications.

Colour Palette

A rich, high-contrast palette built on deep navy and vivid purple — commanding authority while maintaining visual energy and brand distinction across all touchpoints.
